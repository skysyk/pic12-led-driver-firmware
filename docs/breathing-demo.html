Natural breath curve

35% inhale · 6% peak hold · 56% exhale · 3% minimum rest. Total period: 3.0 s.

PWM frequency accepts 489–100,000 Hz. Resolution uses an 8 MHz PIC12 Timer2 model: the smallest fitting 1:1, 1:4, or 1:16 prescaler and the nearest timer period, up to 1,024 duty counts. Within each prescaler range, higher carrier frequencies leave fewer duty counts. Fractional duty models the average of eight adjacent-count dither slots, as in the firmware; turn it off to see hardware-only levels. PWM rounding can move an endpoint by half a selected duty step.

Natural breath is an illustrative modified triangle. Dwell sliders allocate 0–45% of the cycle to each hold; minimum rest is split across the cycle boundary. The remaining time is divided between inhale and exhale in a 5:8 ratio, keeping the total cycle period fixed. Smooth ramps round the corners; linear ramps keep sharp corners. This is a design alternative, not a measured physiological trace.

Exponential follows ThingPulse’s breathing LED formula: (exp(sin(t)) − exp(−1)) / (e − exp(−1)), scaled to the selected brightness limits. The phase is shifted to start at minimum and the time is scaled to the selected cycle period. This curve has a narrower peak and broader trough than sine; its rise and fall are symmetric. Dwell and ramp controls apply to Natural breath. The plot checkboxes control only curve and cursor-dot visibility.

The analytical curves use evenly spaced phase updates and the firmware’s nominal 1.024 ms timebase for cycle length. Actual firmware also skips phases at shorter periods and approximates sine with a table. This preview shows average duty using screen opacity; it does not reproduce carrier flicker, individual dither pulses, calibrated LED output, or calibrated CCT.
